Privacy Policy

App: Little Monsters Word Detective
Effective date: October 13, 2026

Little Monsters Word Detective (the “App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data or other user data.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Diagnostics or crash reports
  • Financial or payment information
  • Photos, files, contacts, calendar information, or other device content

No Third-Party Tracking

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or data brokers.

Data Stored on Your Device

Any information you enter into or create within the App, if applicable, remains on your device. The App does not upload this information to a server or make it available to the developer.

Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the App’s data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The revised policy will be made available through the App Store listing or within the App.
